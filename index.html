About

I'm a designer based just north of Atlanta, Georgia. I've spent my career building brands, products, and interfaces, including work for some of the world's most recognizable brands.

Today I lead design and creative at ClickFunnels and co-founded Overskill, where I head up design. Away from the screen, you'll find me outdoors, tinkering with new tech and AI, or hunting down gear that makes everyday life a little easier.
